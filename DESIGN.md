# Portfolio identity

The public portfolio is an experience of real work: warm paper #fbf8f2, charcoal #211f1b, restrained copper #97412c. Headings use local Space Grotesk, body uses local Sarabun. Real production screenshots carry the project gallery. One full-width featured project leads into a two-column gallery, collapsing to one column on mobile. No hover-only previews or autoplay carousel.

Shared navigation, footer, accessible focus and tokens connect the homepage, work, credentials, contact and case studies. Operational demos retain their useful controls with the same warm palette. Website work and analytics both belong in the introduction. Client claims remain evidence-based; no invented business results.

The badge remains an independent transparent, fixed-length pendulum using the user's genuine photograph. Its face must never be regenerated. Motion is understated and honors reduced-motion preferences.

## Services

The homepage now leads with Thai website design and AI training offers. Order: service introduction, explicit deliverables, live client websites, illustrative AI lesson with a reusable prompt, real automation/credential links, working process, supporting dashboard and toolkit. Two service-specific contact routes preselect the topic and preserve user-written subjects. No learner counts, testimonials or outcome claims are invented.

## Scroll studio

A single sticky Three.js workspace follows the introduction. A modeled graphite/aluminium laptop shows the actual three client screenshots; the camera then moves to explicitly labelled teaching notes and a printed Slack-to-Drive automation diagram, before a final service choice. Scroll progress controls the camera in both directions. Real HTML retains all text and contact routes. Reduced motion collapses the sequence into static chapters. No generated face or invented testimonial is used.
